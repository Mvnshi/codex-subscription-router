GOOD QUESTIONS

A few things
to know.

More detail lives in the documentation.

Is Router actually free?+

Yes. The router is free and open source under the MIT license. It does not include a ChatGPT subscription; you connect plans you already have.

Does it give me unlimited usage?+

No. Each subscription keeps its own limits. Router helps use available quota across your connected accounts. When all accounts are depleted, it shows a combined limit alert.

Will it use my reset credits automatically?+

By default, no. “Ask me” requires confirmation before you spend a reset. You can opt into “Use automatically” from the account menu; ordinary account failover comes first. Credits belong to their account and are never transferred.

Is this an official OpenAI app?+

No. This is an independent community project, unaffiliated with OpenAI. It builds a separate, locally patched desktop app from your existing official installation. Review the source and the terms governing your subscriptions.

What happens when the official app updates?+

Router is tied to known desktop builds. The patcher checks the official app’s version and structure and stops if they don’t match. Compatibility updates are maintained in the public repository; check the notes before rebuilding.
